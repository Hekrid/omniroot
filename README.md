# OmniRoot Privacy Policy

*Effective 17 October 2026*

OmniRoot is an app for Root communities: leveling, moderation, polls, giveaways and other community tools. It keeps only what those features need, stores it inside the Root community that installed it, and never sells data, shows ads or tracks you outside Root.

## What OmniRoot stores

OmniRoot identifies members by their Root user ID and display name. It does not receive your email address, password, phone number or payment details.

| Data | Why it is kept | How long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Display name, XP, level, message count, time of last XP | Leveling and the leaderboard | Until community admins reset XP or remove OmniRoot |
| Warnings, mutes, kicks, bans: who, which moderator, the reason they typed, when | Moderation history and the warning limit | Until OmniRoot is removed (pardoned warnings are kept, marked as pardoned) |
| Current mutes and their end time | Ending mutes on time | Until the mute ends or is lifted |
| Votes in polls, entries in giveaways, giveaway winners | Running polls and giveaways | Until a moderator deletes the poll or giveaway, or OmniRoot is removed |
| Which members were active on each day, and daily totals (messages, joins, leaves, voice minutes) | Community statistics | 90 days |
| Who is in a voice channel and since when | Voice XP | Only while the member is in the channel |
| Owner of a temporary voice channel | Removing the channel once empty | Until the channel is removed |
| IDs of starred messages | Not reposting a message to the starboard twice | Until OmniRoot is removed |
| Settings chosen by community admins (welcome text, custom commands, announcements, feed links) | Running those features | Until changed or OmniRoot is removed |

## Messages

OmniRoot reads messages only in the channels a community has assigned to it. It reads them the moment they are sent, to:

- answer commands such as /rank or /warn
- give XP and count messages for statistics
- check them against the community's auto-mod rules
- count reactions for the starboard

OmniRoot does not keep the text of messages. Two exceptions are visible to the community: the reason a moderator types for an action is saved in the moderation history, and a message that reaches the starboard is reposted, as a quote, in the community's starboard channel. Auto-mod records which rule a message broke, not the message itself.

OmniRoot also sees reactions, members joining and leaving, and who joins or leaves voice channels, for the features listed above.

## Who can see it

Each community's OmniRoot data is separate and stays with that community. It is stored by Root, which hosts the app, under [Root's own privacy policy](https://www.rootapp.com/privacy-policy).

| Who | What they can see |
| --- | --- |
| Members of the community | The leaderboard (names, levels, XP), poll results, giveaway winners, and messages OmniRoot posts in channels |
| Moderators | The above, plus private alerts about auto-mod actions and warning limits |
| Community admins and the managers they choose | Everything in the table above, in OmniRoot's dashboard, including the moderation history and statistics. They can export it as a backup. |

OmniRoot does not sell, rent or share data with anyone else, and has no advertising or analytics. When admins add news or YouTube feeds, OmniRoot fetches those public feeds from the internet; no member data is sent with those requests.

## Deleting data and your rights

OmniRoot processes this data so that the features a community turned on can work, which is the legitimate interest of that community and its members.

- **Ask the community first.** Admins can reset your XP, pardon warnings and delete polls or giveaways from the dashboard.
- **Removing OmniRoot deletes everything.** When a community removes the app, Root deletes all of its OmniRoot data.
- **Contact us** to see, correct or delete the data OmniRoot holds about you, or to object to how it is used. We reply within 30 days.

If you are in the European Union, you can also complain to your data protection authority (in Italy, the [Garante per la protezione dei dati personali](https://www.garanteprivacy.it)).

## Children

OmniRoot is for Root communities and follows Root's age rules. It does not knowingly collect anything extra about children and does not ask anyone's age.
